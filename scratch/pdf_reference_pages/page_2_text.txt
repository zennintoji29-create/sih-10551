Our Idea :
Helix House
GNSS signals are vulnerable to jamming, spoofing, and 
blocking. In battlefields and tough terrains, this leaves 
soldiers without reliable navigation, putting missions and 
lives at risk
ZyraNav is an AI-powered device with a three-layer approach 
ensuring reliable navigation when GPS fails. It uses simulated LEO 
signals to train adaptive algorithms for robust operation in any 
scenario.
•
When GNSS fails, the system seamlessly 
falls back to 3-layer architecture — 
GNSS (primary), LEO SoO (secondary), 
and inertial navigation system-INS 
(tertiary).
•
A smart simulation framework 
generates synthetic LEO SoO signals from 
GNSS data to train and validate cognitive 
algorithms.
•
An AI-enabled cognitive algorithm and 
a Low-cost handheld device with smart 
power management for 24-hour 
continuous operation.
•
India’s first 3-layer navigation 
system to get PNT
•
Transforms non-PNT LEO satellite 
signals into usable PNT data through 
adaptive signal conversion.
•
AI-powered adaptive cognitive 
algorithm detects GNSS failure, switches 
between LEO and INS, and performs 
adaptive signal processing for high 
accuracy.
•
Lab-based Smart Signal Simulation 
Framework lowers development cost and 
trains the cognitive model for real-world 
robustness.
